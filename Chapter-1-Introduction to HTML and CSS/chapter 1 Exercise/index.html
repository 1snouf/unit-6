Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Figure 1: Creamy Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a creamy and delicious dish made with pasta, chicken, cheese, and Alfredo sauce. It is easy to prepare and perfect for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of pasta
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 cup of cooking cream
  • 1/2 cup of grated cheese
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until it becomes soft.
  2. Cut the chicken breast into small pieces.
  3. Cook the chicken in a pan with butter until it turns golden.
  4. Add garlic and stir for one minute.
  5. Add cooking cream and grated cheese to make the sauce.
  6. Mix the cooked pasta with the chicken and Alfredo sauce.
  7. Add salt and pepper, then serve the pasta while it is hot.

Additional Information

This Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a popular Italian-inspired dish known for its rich and creamy flavor. It is perfect for family meals and can be customized by adding vegetables such as broccoli or mushrooms. The recipe is simple to follow, making it suitable for beginners who are learning how to cook. Serving this dish with garlic bread or a fresh salad can make the meal even more enjoyable.
