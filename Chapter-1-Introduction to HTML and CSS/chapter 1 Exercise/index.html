Chicken Alfredo Pasta Recipe

Web Development Student - Chapter 1 Exercise

About This Recipe

Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a classic Italian-American dish that's creamy, delicious, and surprisingly easy to make at home. This rich and comforting pasta features tender chicken pieces in a velvety sauce made with heavy cream, butter, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a cozy dinner or when you want to impress your guests with restaurant-quality flavor right from your own kitchen!

Required Ingredients

  • 2 cups of pasta (fettuccine recommended)
  • 1 chicken breast (boneless, skinless)
  • 1 cup of heavy cooking cream
  • 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon of unsalted butter
  • 1 clove of fresh garlic, minced
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Fill a large pot with water, add salt, and bring to boil
  2. Cook pasta according to package directions until al dente
  3. While pasta cooks, cut chicken into bite-sized pieces
  4. Heat butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat
  5. Cook chicken pieces until golden brown and fully cooked
  6. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant
  7. Pour in cream and add Parmesan cheese, stirring constantly
  8. Simmer sauce until it thickens slightly (2-3 minutes)
  9. Drain pasta and add to the skillet with sauce
  10. Toss everything together and season with salt and pepper
  11. Serve immediately while hot, garnished with fresh parsley

Chef's Tips

For the best results, use freshly grated Parmesan cheese instead of pre-shredded varieties, as it melts better and provides superior flavor. Don't overcook the pasta - it should be al dente (firm to the bite) since it will continue cooking slightly when mixed with the hot sauce. You can add vegetables like broccoli or mushrooms for extra nutrition and flavor!
