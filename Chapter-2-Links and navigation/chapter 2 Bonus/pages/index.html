Welcome to The Last of Us

The Last of Us is a post-apocalyptic television series based on the popular video game. The story follows Joel, a survivor living in a dangerous world after a global infection has destroyed normal society. He is given the task of protecting Ellie, a teenage girl who may be important to finding a cure.

  • Cast: Meet the actors and creative team
  • Characters: Get to know the main characters
