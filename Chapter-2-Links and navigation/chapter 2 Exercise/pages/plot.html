Plot Overview

The Last of Us is a post-apocalyptic television series based on the popular video game. The story follows Joel, a survivor living in a dangerous world after a global infection has destroyed normal society. He is given the task of protecting Ellie, a teenage girl who may be important to finding a cure. As they travel together, they face infected creatures, dangerous people, and emotional challenges.
