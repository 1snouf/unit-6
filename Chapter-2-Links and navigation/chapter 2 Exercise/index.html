The Plot

The Last of Us is about Joel and Ellie surviving in a post-apocalyptic world.

What happens

Joel is a smuggler who is hired to take Ellie, a teenage girl, across the country to a group called the Fireflies.

They face many challenges and must make difficult decisions to survive.

In the end, Joel succeeds in protecting Ellie, but at a great personal cost.

The End

It is a very sad ending but Ellie is safe.
